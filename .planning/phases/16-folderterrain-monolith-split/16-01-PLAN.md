(no I/O, no React, no DB) terrain geometry/transform module — into four cohesive
sub-modules each under ~400 lines, and reduce `folderTerrain.ts` itself to a thin
barrel that re-exports them. This is SPLIT-01 of REF-01: a behavior-preserving
structural refactor. Nothing the workshop shows may change.

pinning tests and many callers import from `../folderTerrain` / `@/app/(dashboard)/access-analysis/folderTerrain`:

(server loader → TEST-02), `FolderPermissionTerrain.tsx` (the panel), plus
`folderInheritance.ts`, `templateFolderTerrain.ts`, `templateView.ts`,
`templateRoleTree.ts`, and `permissionAccess.ts`. Renaming or moving it would break
those imports and violate the byte-identical-test contract.

Purpose: shrink the monolith's merge-conflict surface and make the geometry
independently testable, without touching behavior. Foundation for future
`lib/acc` boundary cleanup (deferred — NOT in this phase).
Output: 4 new pure modules + 1 barrel; all pinning tests byte-identical & green.
</objective>
