  - **Aggregate-bound loader test** → 02-T1 (`provisionedModulesView.test.ts`, rows bounded by
    n_projects × n_modules, never per-member rows).

    + 03-T1 (`projectActivityCounts`).

    `lib/acc/activityClassification.test.ts`: service wins where decisive, verb fallback,
    umbrella deference, unmapped rescue).
